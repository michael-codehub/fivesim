5imulites — install (Windows)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 · the mods
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Copy EVERYTHING inside the "Mods" folder here into:
      Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\Mods\

In the game: Settings > Game Options > Other >
      tick "Enable Custom Content and Mods" + "Script Mods Allowed"
      then FULLY RESTART the game.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2 · the app
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Double-click  5imulites-Host.exe  (it's right here in this folder).
A window with the 5imulites logo opens. Then:
   1) paste your OpenRouter key   (get one free at openrouter.ai)
   2) pick a model
   3) load a household in the game, click "Load Sims from game",
      assign each AI to a Sim
   4) press  "Save & Start the AI"

That's it — the 5 models now play their Sims. You can also click any
Sim in-game > "5imulites: Let AI Play" to hand it to a free agent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Trouble?
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
In-game press Ctrl+Shift+C and type:  fivesim.debug
The app must be open (it runs the local bridge the game talks to).
